Meet Luke

Give him a budget. He handles the heating.

You decide

  • How much to spend a week
  • How warm rooms should be
  • A repairs allowance

Luke does

  • Heats rooms before you arrive
  • Uses cheap, clean hours
  • Stays within your budget
  • Spots a broken radiator and books a fix

He always asks before he spends money or changes a room.

Luke is the helper inside the app. You set the money and the rules once. He does the rest: warms rooms just before you get there, heats when power is cheap and clean, keeps to the budget, and if a radiator stops working he notices and offers to book an engineer from your repairs allowance. He never acts without asking first.
