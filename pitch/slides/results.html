In a simulated five-room house over a week, Heatwise spent 15% less than a normal timer, with 13% less CO₂, and kept people comfortable 97% of the time instead of 70%. The timer only looks cheap because it leaves people cold. So we also tested against heating left on all the time, which keeps people just as warm: Heatwise is still 16% cheaper with 21% less CO₂. The saving grows with spare rooms, because those are the rooms a timer heats for nobody.
