The app

Six tabs, one job: warm rooms, lower bills

Home

Every room’s temperature right now, plus Luke’s week.

Plan

What each room will do today, and why.

Budget

Your weekly money and the rules Luke follows.

Impact

Money and CO₂ saved compared with a timer.

Simulation

A live house: where people are, hour by hour.

Ask Luke

Chat in plain words: “make the living room 22”.

This is the app. Home shows every room now. Plan shows what each room will do today and why. Budget is where you set your weekly money and rules. Impact shows what you saved against a normal timer. Simulation is a live pretend house so you can watch it work. And Ask Luke is a chat with the assistant.
