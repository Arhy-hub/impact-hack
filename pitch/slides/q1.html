Hands up

Which use of energy causes the most CO₂?

Driving

Cars, vans and lorries

Flying

Planes and air freight

Electricity

Lights, screens and gadgets

Heat

Warming homes, water and industry

Start with a show of hands. Read the question, then go through the four options and ask people to put their hand up for the one they think is biggest. Most people pick driving or flying. Click once to reveal: it’s heat.
