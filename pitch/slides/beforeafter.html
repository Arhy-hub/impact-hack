Heat follows people

Blue: cool or eco · Orange to red: heated

Same house, same three people, same day. On the left, the old way: a timer heats all six rooms, even at midday when everyone is out. On the right, Heatwise: the bathroom is warm for 06:45, the kitchen for breakfast, everything drops to a 12° safety level when the house is empty, and the living room is ready for the evening. The spare room is never used, so it stays cool.
